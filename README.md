# FitQuest

FitQuest is your go-to guide for mastering fitness exercises safely and effectively. Whether you're just starting your fitness journey or looking to take your workouts to the next level, FitQuest offers clear, step-by-step guides for a wide range of exercises and tailored workout programs.

## Features

### Explore Our Workout Guides
Browse detailed workout guides by muscle group:
- **Chest:** Flat Barbell Bench Press, Dumbbell Bench Press, Incline Barbell Bench Press, Dips, and more.
- **Back:** Bent Over Row, Lat Pulldown, Pullups, etc.
- **Arms:** Dumbbell Curl, Tricep Pushdown, Hammer Curl, etc.
- **Shoulders:** Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press, Dumbbell Lateral Raise, Machine Shoulder Press, etc.
- **Quadriceps:** Leg Extension, Leg Press, Squat, etc.
- **Hamstrings:** Seated Leg Curl, Romanian Deadlift, Lying Leg Curl, etc.

### Tailored Workout Programs
Stay on track with structured programs designed for different fitness goals:
- **Full Body Workout:** 45 min, high intensity; strength training and cardio for all major groups.
- **Upper Body Strength:** 30 min, moderate intensity; chest, back, shoulders, arms.
- **Core Conditioning:** 25 min, moderate intensity; planks, Russian twists, leg raises, more.
- **Leg Day:** 40 min, high intensity; squats, deadlifts, lunges, and stretches.

Each program includes:
- Warm-up routines
- Detailed exercise breakdown
- Cool down instructions
- Benefits and recommended fitness levels
- Weekly schedule suggestions


#### Motivation
Our shared passion for health and wellness led us to create FitQuest—a platform offering easy access to quality workout resources and personalized programs, supporting everyone on their fitness journey.

## Getting Started

To run FitQuest locally:

1. Clone the repo:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/KaungJr/FitQuest.git
   ```
2. Open `index.html` in your web browser.

No build or server required.
